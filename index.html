DM a Donut Console

Website constructed for DM a Donut, a ministry where people send in their questions about Christianity, faith & life, and we answer them and give them a free donut!

The website interacts with the Instagram messaging API to receive questions, automatically sort and organise them. It also enable automatic sending of messages, and printing of questions, and it exposes a public interface to students answering questions so they can message the person without having access to the full admin console or Instagram account.
